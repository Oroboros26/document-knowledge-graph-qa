# Supply Agreement SA-2025-014 (synthetic)

Page 1. This agreement is made between Aurora Gear AG ("Aurora") and Kestrel Textiles s.r.o. ("Kestrel", also "KT") effective 1 March 2025.

Page 2. Kestrel shall supply Ripstop-40 fabric to Aurora. All Ripstop-40 fabric is produced at the Brno Plant, which Kestrel operates.

Page 3. Aurora may audit the Brno Plant once per year. Kestrel must notify Aurora of any process deviation within 5 business days.
